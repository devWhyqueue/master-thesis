\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}
Table~\ref{tab:contrast} reports the six primary contrasts at $\rho=100$. At least one contrast is not noninferior, so omitting LoRA is not supported for the full tested scope; the individual outcomes stand on their own.
The CE controls reach BRACS 31.17 (frozen) and 31.57 (adapted), TCGA-UT 67.32 (frozen) and 69.95 (adapted) balanced accuracy.
