# Experiments

Controlled patch-classification studies on frozen Virchow2 features. Each study builds on the previous one. The headline question: why does patient shortage hurt accuracy, and what recovers it?

## Layout

- `NN_name/code/`: CLI (`__main__.py`) plus a study package. `configs/`: run configs. `tests/`: unit tests.
- `NN_name/report/`: `N_name.tex` (protocol), `results.tex` (results, discussion), compiled PDF.
- `environment.def`, `requirements-experiment.txt`: shared Apptainer image for Hydra runs (see `CLUSTER.md`).
- Datasets: BRACS and TCGA-UT (02–05, 18); TCGA-UT only (06–17). Readout: logistic regression unless noted.
- Effects are patient-macro balanced accuracy in percentage points. Intervals come from a paired test-patient bootstrap. Practical threshold: 1 pp.

## Open thread

Mechanism (exp-16/17, TCGA-UT): class-centre error (first moment) carries ~3/4 of the patient benefit, whitening along population between-patient directions (second moment) a further quarter; close to additive, together closing the gap. Pool centres and pool basis use all training patients — mechanism, not remedy; only the cohort's own basis is a remedy (+1.3–2.3 pp, 0.11 of the gap). Exp-18 (BRACS): centre channel transfers (share 0.82, noise ratio 0.99), whitening channel does not separate (interaction −1.78 pp at G = 5), cohort basis gains nothing — treat second-moment remedies as cohort-specific. Exp-19: first-moment mitigation from the cohort alone fails (see table) — a working remedy must move centres outside the span of the cohort's own class centres and must separate error from separation inside it, so it needs information from outside the labelled cohort (related classes, unlabelled slides, population covariance). Exp-20: directional shrinkage along the cohort basis gains +1.29 pp at G = 5 on TCGA-UT (nothing on BRACS) but does not reduce centre error — it acts like cohort whitening and stays below it, so the centre headroom is still unrecovered from the cohort alone. Exp-21: training against the cohort's centre uncertainty (patient-covariance ISDA loss) gains +1.28 pp at G = 5 on TCGA-UT (nothing on BRACS), equal to directional shrinkage and below cohort whitening — three remedies on the same cohort covariance converge; the cohort tells which directions not to trust, not where its centres are. Next: (1) external shrinkage targets; (2) predict accuracy at untested G (e.g. 40) from the no-fit centre risk ρ_G = Σ_c Σ_{k≠c} (w_c − w_k)ᵀ Σ_c (w_c − w_k) / G, with BRACS as second calibration point. One patient count per classifier is the standard design.
