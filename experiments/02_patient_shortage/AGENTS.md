| 19 | centre_shrinkage | Cohort improves own class centres? James-Stein shrinkage toward cohort grand mean, closed form (S) + validation-tuned (St), G = 5/10/20, TCGA-UT + BRACS; R/C reused from exp-16/18 | No. TCGA-UT: S5 -0.30 pp [-0.58, -0.03], St5 +0.14 pp; recovery of C-R headroom -0.028 at G = 5, 0.024 at G = 10; shrinkage share -0.05. Shrinkage removes 21% (TCGA-UT), 27% (BRACS) of squared centre error at G = 5, but only 33%/40% of that error radial, and radial direction = class's own discriminative axis. alpha = 4 never selected. |
| 20 | directional_shrinkage | Shrink class centres along cohort's own between-patient eigenbasis (strong on noisy, weak on class-separating directions) recovers headroom radial shrinkage misses? Closed form (A) + validation-tuned (At), G = 5/10/20, TCGA-UT + BRACS; R/C/S/St/RWc reused | Success on TCGA-UT only. At5 +1.29 pp [0.99, 1.57] (A5 +1.03), recovery 0.121 [0.093, 0.148], gap share 0.09; beats St by 1.14 pp, trails RWc by 0.97 pp. BRACS: At5 +0.36 pp [-0.31, 1.11], inconclusive, trails radial shrinkage. Gain not better centres: A removes only 4% of squared centre error, selected alpha raises it 33-62% on TCGA-UT, per-draw gain uncorrelated with error reduction; reads as first-moment whitening. alpha = 4 chosen 12/30 TCGA-UT fits at G = 20. |
| 21 | centre_uncertainty | Training against cohort's own class-centre uncertainty V_G = Σ/G (ISDA upper bound on expected cross-entropy, features unchanged) recovers headroom? Fixed t = 1 (U), validation-tuned t ∈ {0, 0.25, 1, 4, 16, 64, 256} (Ut), trace-matched isotropic control (It, t grid extended to 4096), G = 5/10/20, TCGA-UT + BRACS; R/C/S/St/A/At/RWc reused | Success on TCGA-UT only. Ut5 +2.34 pp [2.03, 2.63] (U5 +0.52), recovery 0.220 [0.191, 0.251], 5→20 gap reduction 1.02 pp [0.71, 1.34] (share 0.109). It5 +0.16 pp [−0.01, 0.31] (It selects t = 256 on TCGA-UT, 1024 on BRACS, top value 4096 in ≤4/30 fits; interior), so gain needs patient directions. Ut ≈ RWc (≤0.08 pp every G), beats At by 1.06 pp, St by 2.20 pp at G = 5. Selected t = 16 or 64 in 90/90 TCGA-UT fits (interior), i.e. 16–64× modelled variance. BRACS: Ut5 +0.25 pp [−0.37, 0.97], no interval excludes zero at any G. 538/11,340 (TCGA-UT), 256/11,340 (BRACS) fits hit iteration limit, all isotropic (excluded). |
| 22 | spectrum_regularization | Cohort's patient directions useful but eigenvalues too noisy? | 23 | within_patient_span | Patch residuals of same G patients reveal patient directions their means miss?
